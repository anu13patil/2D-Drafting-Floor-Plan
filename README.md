# 🏠 Final House Plan – 2D & 3D CAD Design

A residential house plan designed in **Autodesk Fusion** and **AutoCAD (DWG)**, including a dimensioned 2D floor plan drawing and a 3D model.

---

## 📐 Project Overview

This project contains the complete design of a single-storey house with a rectangular footprint of approximately **10,800 mm × 8,200 mm** (about 10.8 m × 8.2 m). It includes:

- A **2D floor plan** with walls, openings, furniture layout and dimensions
- A **top/elevation view** drawn above the plan, aligned to the same grid
- A **3D model** of the house
- A drawing sheet with a title block and grid references (columns 1–8, rows A–F)

## 🧱 Layout Highlights

- **Two bedrooms** along the top of the plan, separated by an internal partition wall
- **Small utility / bathroom area** at the top right with fixtures
- **Central corridor** connecting the rooms
- **Living and dining area** at the bottom left with seating, a dining table and chairs
- **Kitchen** at the bottom right with a counter and sink units
- Door and window openings marked in the exterior and interior walls

## 🚀 How to View

1. **2D plan:** open `2d_final_house_plan_Drawing_code_alpha.pdf` in any PDF viewer.
2. **3D model (online):** click the Fusion link above, no installation required.
3. **3D model (offline):** open `3d_final_house_plan.dwg` in AutoCAD, Fusion, or a free viewer such as Autodesk Viewer, DWG TrueView, or LibreCAD (2D only).
